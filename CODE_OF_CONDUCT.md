# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and users are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith, and focus criticism on the work rather than people.

## Research and clinical-data boundary

Do not post patient identifiers, clinical records, credentials, private device data, unpublished vulnerability details, or other sensitive information in issues, pull requests, discussions, or commits.

## Security reports

For suspected security issues, follow the repository's `SECURITY.md` policy rather than publishing exploit details in a public issue.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct violates this standard.
